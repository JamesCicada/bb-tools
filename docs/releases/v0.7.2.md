# BB Tools v0.7.2

- **Gem slots on spawned weapons:** weapons created directly at an upgrade level keep closed blood-gem slots. Other item-grant mods do the same, so this is native game behavior. Items → **Open gem slots on upgraded weapons** (on by default) now spawns an upgraded weapon one level lower. Upgrade it once in the workshop to reach the requested level and open every slot; **Free weapon upgrades** removes the cost. Goods, armor, runes and +0 weapons are unchanged.
- **Linux connection message:** if the character is not loaded yet when you connect (for example at the title screen or while loading), Linux now says the player may be loading, matching Windows, instead of a raw `Bad address` read error. Reported on Steam Deck.
- Nexus Mods now receives the Linux download automatically with each release, alongside the Windows file.

## Testing and compatibility

Both fixes are **NOT TESTED IN-GAME**. Thanks to the Nexus reporter who found that upgrading a +9 weapon to +10 opens all slots. Steam Deck users who still see the read error with a character loaded: please attach the session log (its path is shown in Settings) and your bbport version.
