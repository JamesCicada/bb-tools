# BB Tools v0.6.8

- **NG-cycle editor:** view and edit the playthrough counter from Player, with current-character validation and write verification. 0 = NG, 1 = NG+; native difficulty scaling caps at NG+6. This does not reset quests or begin a new journey.
- **Optional experimental camera and noclip:** enable Settings → Experimental to reveal Photo studio and Camera → Movement flight/noclip. The preference defaults off and never starts a mode automatically. Camera bindings are configurable in the same tab.
- Keep tool-allocated photo frames out of the native owning pointer, avoiding game-destructor ownership errors during camera teardown.
- Strengthen the photo hunter lock with native logic, movement and gravity gates; restore the original HUD visibility; cancel pending camera preparation on exit; prevent native action requests while the hunter is frozen.
- Tighten flight/noclip actor ownership, return-point checks and movement timing. Exit/opt-out restores owned state and retries cleanup through temporary loading failures.

**NG editing and photo/free camera/flight/noclip are NOT TESTED IN-GAME.** They remain labeled experimental and need feedback on Bloodborne PC and shadPS4. Synthetic native/UI checks establish state handling, not gameplay correctness. Shoulder-camera and blood-gem tools retain their existing pending-live-test status.

The separate **0.6.8-linux-beta.1** remains **BETA — NOT TESTED IN-GAME** and receives shared translations/version alignment. It does not include these Windows tools.

Windows ZIP: `BBTools.exe` and `README.md` only.
