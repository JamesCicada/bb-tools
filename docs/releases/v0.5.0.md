# BB Tools v0.5.0

Bloodborne PC and shadPS4 are officially supported for CUSA03173 1.09.
shadPS4 support is tested and confirmed; no experimental opt-in is required.
Prepared as a draft for review before publication.

## Highlights

- Native inventory weapon repair with per-slot before/after reports, threshold auto-repair and keep-full controls. Both-hand live confirmation remains pending.
- Player movement speed, world gravity, flight, HUD visibility and active slow/rapid poison cure.
- Searchable item-name import from your own game files, expanded movement/gravity/HUD presets and connection-bound chalice bookmarks.
- Experimental Micolash, Gehrman and Moon Presence revival profiles.
- Compatibility guide, live test checklist and simplified Nexus description.

## Adjustments and limitations

- Noclip and free camera are disabled and hidden. Entity inspection is hidden pending realtime targeted-enemy updates.
- Ending-boss revival can alter story/NG progression. Camera and exploration overrides can cause visual or performance issues. Use a separate practice save.
- Chalice coordinate bookmarks expire across reconnects or dungeon progress/context changes.
- New features retain their individual live-test status in [TODO](https://github.com/JamesCicada/bb-tools/blob/v0.5.0/TODO.md); automated checks do not establish live-game confirmation.

## Package

Portable, self-contained Windows x64 ZIP containing only `BBTools.exe` and `README.md`.
The SHA-256 checksum is supplied separately. No game files are included.
